# Narrator bible

Who the narrator is, across every video. The narrator is the channel's creator, speaking in their own cloned voice, so everything personal said on air must be true. Read this before writing any narrator line; never contradict it. Only the creator adds or changes facts here. The skill updates the anecdote log only after a script is approved.

## On air: facts the narrator may mention

Use one or two personal touches per video at most, and only where they fit naturally.

- **Where I read.** [creator's answer]
- **How I read.** [creator's answer]
- **Writers I love:** [creator's answer]

How to use them:

- General habits can be said as they are.
- Anything about a **specific** book, such as when or where it was read, how it felt or what was underlined, is true only if the creator confirms it. In drafts, write it as `[CONFIRM: …]` (see "Truth rules").
- A favourite writer may be mentioned when the link is real. Do not invent an opinion about a writer.

## Truth rules

1. A personal line not covered by this file is written as `[CONFIRM: the line as it would be spoken]` in `05-narrator.md` and `03-script.md`. Delivery lists every one.
2. The creator confirms it (the brackets are removed), rewrites it, or deletes it. A script with any `[CONFIRM: …]` left is not approved, and no speech is generated for it.
3. When a script is approved, each personal line used goes into the anecdote log below. A new general fact the creator confirmed goes into "On air".
4. Never reuse a logged anecdote in another video, and never contradict one.
